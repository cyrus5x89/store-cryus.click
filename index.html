How it works

  1. 1

    Brief. Send the product, goal, length, platform, deadline, and either your footage or your preferred AI model.

  2. 2

    Idea. We send 1-2 directions (hook, script, shot list) within 24 hours.

  3. 3

    Approve. You pick a direction and pay a 50% deposit to start. Check the script and shots carefully before approving, because later changes cost extra.

  4. 4

    Produce. We edit, or generate the AI video. For AI work, one test take goes to you first.

  5. 5

    Review. Regular video includes 2 revision rounds; round 3 is charged extra. AI video revisions are charged by length or difficulty, so we agree on the details before starting.

  6. 6

    Deliver. Final files by Google Drive link. The balance is due on delivery.
