How it works

  1. Brief. Send the product, goal, length, platform, deadline, and either your footage or your preferred AI model.
  2. Idea. We send 1-2 directions (hook, script, shot list) within 24 hours.
  3. Approve. You pick a direction and pay a 50% deposit to start.
  4. Produce. We edit, or generate the AI video. For AI work, one test take goes to you first.
  5. Review. 2 rounds of revisions included.
  6. Deliver. Final files by Google Drive link. The balance is due on delivery.

Standard turnaround: 3-5 days. Rush (48 h): +50%.
